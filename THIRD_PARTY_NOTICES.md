## Provenance to verify before redistribution

- `assets/audio/slow-stride-loop.ogg` is used as ambient music, but this repository contains no creator, source, or license record for it. Do not treat the track as CC0 based on the art notice; confirm permission and add the attribution/license terms here.
- `icon.png` is used as the site icon, but its source and license are not documented. Confirm its provenance and license before redistributing it.

These items are listed as unresolved rather than assigned a license without evidence.
