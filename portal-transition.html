The Floating Archive · Lean Stepper

We built our own Lean interpreter.

It follows supported Lean expressions one rule at a time. For code beyond this teaching subset, Lean 4 can run the program.

02 · The reduction trace

Every reduction leaves a trace.

The trace keeps each expression in order. Its bright line is the current step. Reduce follows the next rewrite; Previous retraces it.

03 · The live viewer

The viewer gives each step a place.

It turns the current expression into a visual. As the expression changes, the light and camera move with it through the world.

04 · A path through the isles

Each rewrite carries us to a new isle.

The islands form a forest around your journey. The code, trace, and viewer travel with you as the interpreter reduces the expression.
